Method and limits (read before judging)

  • Each clip's patterns come from a packed blob generated from the draft plan, written by the real packed writer, and rendered through real mix_block in the 6c1be4b tree. Event dumps were checked programmatically: 8 bars, correct slots, chords, lead/bass steps, drum lanes, fills only on bars 4 and 8, all caps hold.
  • All clips are deterministic: each WAV is byte-identical across two renders. Snapshot guard passed (7 checks each).
  • Host transposition: the packed writer takes root/scale from the host level, not the blob. D-minor drafts render in A minor (Amen host), the D-major draft in A major (Musicbox host). Harmony shape is preserved, pitch is transposed.
  • Progressions and drums are PROPOSALS from the analysis, not transcriptions — chord loops were not measurable from the Suno mixes, and drum transcription failed. What you hear is the plan, not a verified copy of the original.
  • All tracks share the group-A host tracks, as in Phase 2. Timbre differences come from patterns and register, not per-track presets.
  • Live game-tick timing, FORM section lengths, Motion/FEEL, and per-track presets are not in these clips. These are listening renders, not production levels: no merge, pointer move, or flash follows from them.
