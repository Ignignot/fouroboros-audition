Method and limits (read before judging)

  • Each clip's patterns were captured from the real packed writer on the Phase 1 qualified foundation (6c1be4b), then rendered through real mix_block in the same tree. Event dumps for every bar are in the #31 handoff; all variant isolations check out (only the intended bars differ).
  • All clips are deterministic: each WAV is byte-identical across two renders. Snapshot guard passed (nothing rewrote a pattern mid-bar).
  • Fills are isolated to bars 4 and 8, per the recorded fills brief. In live gameplay, Amen boundary fills would fire on every section change — denser fills are a plan decision, not a silent render choice.
  • Live game-tick timing (FORM section lengths, autopilot, food) is harness-proven, not audio-proven here. Motion, FEEL, SEEDED and tame are not in this tree and are not in these clips.
  • Option D (true second-cycle content) was NOT built: it needs a contract extension and ChatGPT architecture review first, plus Britton confirming development (not just endings) is the gap.
  • Amen i–VI is a test variant. Production stays the vamp unless Britton adopts i–VI. The closing variant is scratch only.
